开始体验

打开 App 后先扫描房间。缓慢观察地面、四周墙面和天花板；覆盖足够空间后自动倒计时。风暴开始后，在已扫描且没有真实障碍物的地面内缓慢移动，侧身躲避或握拳挥击陨石。

扫描迟迟没有完成

检查系统设置中的环境感知权限，保证房间光线充足。向下观察遗漏的地面，并缓慢环顾四周；不要仅靠计时等待扫描结束。

铁拳不显示或命中不稳定

允许手部追踪，让双手位于头显能看见的位置。避免双手互相遮挡或贴近身体；先缓慢握拳，再尝试挥击。若空间追踪中断，请先等待恢复。

声音、暂停与退出

战斗中的空间按钮可以关闭或开启声音、暂停风暴。暂停后可以继续、重新扫描、查看隐私与体验说明或离开空间。也请检查系统音量。

保存的房间

扫描框架只保存在本机。再次使用仍会扫描并核对当前环境；定位成功且布局匹配时可恢复保存框架。重新扫描会替换该记录，删除 App 会移除本机数据。

Getting started

Open the app and scan your room. Slowly look at the floor, surrounding walls and ceiling. Once enough space is covered, a countdown starts the storm. Move slowly within scanned, obstacle-free floor areas to sidestep meteors or strike them with a closed fist.

Scanning will not finish

Enable World Sensing in system Settings and use a well-lit room. Look down at uncovered floor and slowly look around. Scanning depends on coverage, not just elapsed time.

Iron fists are missing or strikes are inconsistent

Enable Hand Tracking and keep your hands visible to the headset. Avoid covering one hand with the other or holding them against your body. Slowly make a fist before striking. If spatial tracking is interrupted, wait for it to recover.

Audio, pause and exit

Spatial controls let you toggle sound or pause the storm. From pause, resume, rescan, view privacy and app information, or exit the space. Check system volume if audio is missing.

Saved rooms

The room framework is saved only on this device. Each session still scans and checks the current environment. A saved framework can be restored when localization and layout checks succeed. Rescanning replaces the record; deleting the app removes local data.

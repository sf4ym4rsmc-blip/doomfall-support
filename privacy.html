1. 开发者与联系方式

2. 空间与手部数据

App 使用系统提供的房间网格、空间定位与头部、手部追踪数据，使虚拟废墟与房间对齐、确定陨石路径并判断躲避和击打。这些数据在设备上处理，不由本 App 上传至开发者或第三方服务器。手部和头部姿态用于当前体验，不由本 App 保存为历史追踪记录。

3. 本机保存与删除

为恢复体验，App 在本机保存扫描的房间框架与定位锚点信息。重新扫描会替换该记录；删除 App 可移除 App 的本机数据。App 可能在本机保留有限的性能与战斗诊断信息，例如处理耗时、场景物体数量、得分及设备温度状态，用于排查问题；这些诊断不包含房间网格或手部姿态，不由 App 自动上传。

4. 账户、广告与分析

游戏无需注册账户，不集成广告、跨 App 追踪或第三方分析 SDK。App 不会向开发者上传房间扫描、手部追踪或游戏诊断数据。

5. 权限与客服

可在系统设置中管理环境感知与手部追踪权限。关闭相应权限会影响扫描或击打功能。如果你主动通过电子邮件联系支持，我们会使用你提供的联系信息与问题描述处理该请求。请不要发送密码、验证码或不必要的房间、个人信息。

6. 外部链接与本政策更新

本支持网站仅提供公开说明，不运行广告或分析脚本；网站托管与邮件服务提供商可能为交付请求及安全目的处理必要的网络信息。本政策更新后会在此页面公布。

1. Developer and contact

This policy applies to Doomfall: Meteor Storm (末日来袭), an Apple Vision Pro game developed by Beijing Yimo Bufan Film And Television Culture Media Co., Ltd. Contact support or ask privacy questions at 553748912@qq.com.

2. Spatial and hand data

The app uses system-provided room meshes, spatial localization, and head and hand tracking to align virtual ruins, place meteor paths, and detect dodges and strikes. This processing happens on your device. The app does not upload this data to the developer or third-party servers. Head and hand poses are used during play and are not saved by the app as a tracking history.

3. Local storage and deletion

The app saves a scanned room framework and localization anchor information on your device to restore the experience. Rescanning replaces the record; deleting the app removes its local data. Limited performance and combat diagnostics may be stored locally, such as processing times, scene object counts, scores and device thermal state. These diagnostics do not contain room meshes or hand poses and are not automatically uploaded by the app.

4. Accounts, advertising and analytics

No game account is required. The game does not integrate advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not upload room scans, hand tracking or game diagnostics to the developer.

5. Permissions and support

You can manage World Sensing and Hand Tracking permissions in system Settings. Disabling them affects scanning or strikes. If you email support, we use the contact information and issue details you provide to handle your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal or room information.

6. External links and updates

This support website provides public information without advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting and email providers may process network information needed to deliver requests and maintain security. Updates to this policy will be posted here.
